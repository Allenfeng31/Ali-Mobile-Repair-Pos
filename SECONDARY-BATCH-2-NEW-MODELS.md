# Secondary Phone Brands Batch 2 — Current / New Model Research

Research date: 2026-10-27. This is discovery only; it does not authorize route, POS, pricing or booking changes.

## Decision rule

A manufacturer/support listing proves that a device exists, not that Ali Mobile has demand, parts, skills, prices or booking authority. Add no Model Hub until the exact Australian variant is present in POS and parts/service support is confirmed. New pages should start quote-led unless numeric authority is separately validated.

## Findings

### Motorola — highest validation priority

Motorola Australia's current navigation lists families absent from Ali Mobile: razr 60/50, Edge 60 Pro/Fusion and Edge 50 variants; moto g06, g86 power 5G, g56 5G, g04s, g35 5G, g05 and g15; moto e14 and ThinkPhone. Source: [Motorola Australia](https://www.motorola.com.au/).

- **P1 POS/parts validation:** moto g56 5G, g86 power 5G, Edge 60 Pro, Edge 60 Fusion, razr 60.
- **P2 demand watch:** g06, g35 5G, g05, g15, g04s, Edge 50 family, razr 50 family.
- **Do not create yet:** all above until exact POS model/variant and repair authority exist.

### Telstra — highest catalogue-gap priority

Telstra's official device-support catalogue lists current/recent branded phones absent from Ali Mobile, including T-Essential, T-Essential 2, T-Inspire, T-Inspire 2, T-Pro, T-Pro 2 5G, T-Prime 5G, T-Elite 5G, T-Elite 2 5G and T-Flip 5G. Sources: [Telstra device support](https://mobilesupport.telstra.com.au/telstra?filter=phones), [Telstra T-Inspire product page](https://www.telstra.com.au/mobile-phones/prepaid-mobiles/telstra-t-inspire).

- **P1 POS/parts validation:** T-Inspire 2, T-Essential 2, T-Pro 2 5G, T-Prime 5G, T-Elite 2 5G.
- **P2 demand watch:** T-Inspire, T-Essential, T-Pro, T-Elite 5G, T-Flip 5G.
- Exact OEM/model codes are required because Telstra-branded hardware may be manufactured by another vendor.

### Huawei — global/current evidence, Australian sales uncertain

Huawei's global phone catalogue includes Pura 80 family, Mate X7/Mate 80 Pro and newer nova families. Huawei Australia's support bulletin also names Pura 80, Mate X7/Mate 80 Pro and nova 13/14 devices, while the Australian product sitemap exposes only a very limited smartphone range. Sources: [Huawei global phones](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/phones/), [Huawei Australia security support](https://consumer.huawei.com/au/support/bulletin/), [Huawei Australia sitemap](https://consumer.huawei.com/au/sitemap/).

- **P2 demand/POS watch:** Pura 80, Pura 80 Pro/Ultra, nova 14/14 Pro, Mate X7, Mate 80 Pro.
- **HOLD:** no page creation until Australian repair demand, exact variants and parts supply are verified.

### Sony — current global models, no verified Australian sales path

Sony's official support inventory identifies Xperia 1 VII and Xperia 10 VII, plus newer Xperia generation entries. Ali Mobile currently stops at Xperia 1 II/5 II/10 II. Source: [Sony Xperia Android-version inventory](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/SX243901).

- **HOLD:** no Australian manufacturer sales listing was verified; require local device demand and parts confirmation.

### HTC — current global model, Australian availability uncertain

HTC officially documents U24 pro and its regional catalogue also carries U23/U23 pro. Source: [HTC U24 pro](https://www.htc.com/eu/smartphones/htc-u24-pro/).

- **P2 demand/POS watch:** HTC U24 pro, U23 pro and U23.
- **HOLD:** no verified official Australian sales/support path; require exact variant, demand and parts evidence.
